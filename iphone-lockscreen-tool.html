Self-check

    Runs entirely on this device: verifies the export size, black-bar trim, all four render modes, a real full-size JPG, the batch-download path, export filenames, the blurred-background path for this browser, and the visible-% math for portrait images. With an image loaded it tests your actual image; without one it uses a built-in test pattern.

    Fill

    No spaces ever. Best when the subject is centered. You lose the far sides on landscape images — check the % above.

    Balanced

    The compromise from your screenshots. Image gets much taller on screen, only the edges trim. Start at 62%.

    Extend

    Keeps 100% of the width. Top/bottom are a flipped continuation of the scene itself, not a blur smear.

    Fit

    100% visible, smallest on screen. Use only if every pixel matters and you don't mind the blur behind it.

    Built from your back-and-forth on Oct 23: cropped → blurred fit (too small) → balanced 62% → full-photo fill (too cropped) → extended scene.
